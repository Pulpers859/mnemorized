## Calibration and Reuse

Method follows the [broadened user standard](chapter-2-cue-audit.md#subsequent-user-calibration).
Familiar category objects, actions, wordplay and partial learned sounds all count;
standalone diagnostic identification and complete syllable coverage are not
requirements. Chapter6's user-supplied rotten-eggs/sulfur/sulfon- calibration is
retained as precedent. No sulfur term was selected here, so the egg incubator
must not be confused with that separate **cracked rotten eggs with stink lines**
primitive. Incubation uses an intact egg and incubator, not stink or sulfur.

Ant/rack, small/fox, plaque/plague and bottle/botul- are deliberately approximate
learned name hooks. They remain provisional without object-naming or recall
tests. Familiar object names may differ between learners. No additional sound
props are added merely to fill gaps. Tularemia remains open to a better proposal;
holding it is not a claim that partial associations are invalid. The prior
doxycycline docks/bicycle attempt remains rejected, not quietly admitted here.

Earlier chapter banks, the catalog, and both sound-bridge workshops were checked
for existing mappings. Four complete candidates are reused. Incubation reuses
the catalog's hourglass primitive in a new composition. The stockpile category
is related to, but not a duplicate of, Chapter5's pushing-cart push-packs cue.
Q fever does not promote the workshop's rejected beaver/fever design.
New associations were composed here without consulting or copying proprietary
symbol catalogs, scenes or signature character systems. No exhaustive novelty
search was performed; generic ideas can overlap existing mnemonic systems.
