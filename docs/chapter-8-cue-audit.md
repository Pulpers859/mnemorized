## Honest Limits

Sulfur mustard reuses Patrick's rotten-egg/sulfur convention. It is a name
composition, not ingredients, chemical production, a smell test or a toxicology
claim. Pool and cleaning bottles require learned context, not blind exact
chemical identification. Blue dye is a partial-name cue, not unique drug ID.
The shield is a category metaphor, not a general claim about antidote actions.
Nothing here validates clinical recommendations in this source, doses, exposure
rules, chemistry, diagnostic criteria or current guidelines.
