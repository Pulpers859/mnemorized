## Method And Calibration

Followed chapter2's calibrated familiar-category and partial-name standard.
Retained candidates need not encode every syllable or uniquely recover a blind
diagnosis. Scope states omitted qualifiers; holds include an actual attempted idea
in `attempted_cue` and a reason. Full procedural statements, quantities, angles,
durations, wordless letter markers and bibliographic scaffolding are excluded
only after their meaningful lexical headwords are screened. No medical facts
were inferred from cue imagery. The JSON contains all per-item emphasis and
source mappings, exclusions, limitations and historical reconsiderations.

Priority was chapter/section/subheadings; equipment and named-condition headwords
from tables31-1 through31-14; principal named-vessel figure labels; and meaningful
lexical headwords from bold sentences. This is not every unformatted body word,
every table verb/comment, every ancillary anatomy label or every ultrasound
raster abbreviation. Generic narrative site names were not expanded simply to
increase the count. Repeated headings and aliases map to entries without
increasing trial counts.
