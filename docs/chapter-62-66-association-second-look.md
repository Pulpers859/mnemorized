# Chapters 62-66: Association Second Look

## What Changed

The previous seven-new-candidate headline counted visual ingredient families.
It was not a count of all useful term-to-image associations. The original
1,188-record batch has NOT been fully reclassified. This bounded second look
examined 29 promising or comparative records under the unchanged broad rubric.
It is not an exhaustive creative search or a corrected whole-batch yield.

Two authors proposed records; a separate non-author AI reviewed all 29 against
their originals and named prior records. Main adjudicated the exceptions. These
are editorial judgments, not human-expert or empirical validation. First author
hashes, findings and disagreements are in the [independent review](chapter-62-66-association-second-look-review.md).

## Existing Value Previously Hidden By Prop Counts

BUN/bun differs from hypercarbia's carb-food/excess bridge. Toothbrush/oral
cleaning differs from paintbrush/brushstroke. Calculator/Cal- differs from
adding-machine/Add-. Other examples include grave/grav-, bar/Barr-, diffuse ink,
pasture, rapping, lens/lenz-, gem/Gem-, western/wester-, greeting/hi and mist/MIST.
Gemella's prior amethyst/amy- reading is explicitly an inference, not a quotation
of that older record's scope. Shared props are not automatically shared bridges.

Conversely, chapter 66's toothbrush and empty-pitcher empyema repeat established
oral-cleaning and empty/emp- bridges. A term, chapter or prop-subtype change alone
does not justify counting fresh creative yield.
