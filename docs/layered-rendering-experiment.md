# Layered Rendering Pilot

Date: 2026-10-15. Status: experimental; app defaults unchanged.

Follow-up: the separate [reference-conditioned full-scene trial](reference-conditioned-rendering-experiment.md)
reached100/100from both auditors after two complete renders plus one background.
Its source-corrected lesson and associations differ from this frozen pilot, so it
does not revise the scores below or establish a controlled superiority comparison.

## Question

Can an empty background plus separately generated mnemonic interaction groups,
positioned by deterministic software, improve full-scene rendering and repair?

Patrick authorized a comparison against the existing eight-anchor Forge scene.
The exact v9 bundle and baseline image were frozen. Background prompt and all
eight VISUAL descriptions were extracted verbatim; only asset-delivery instructions
were added. Existing source/association defects remain in both arms. This was not
a fresh Forge lesson, a source repair, or a publishable clinical-content trial.

## Procedure

Artifacts: `Troubleshooting Prompts/layered_comparison_2026-10-15/` (ignored).
All images came from Antigravity's supported native chat/image lane. Antigravity
did not grade its output. The full Constitution was required for every task.

- Baseline: one existing whole-scene native render, 1376x768.
- Initial layered arm: one background plus eight independent group renders.
- Two groups had native duplicate/corrupt output and were regenerated separately.
- Compositor: deterministic masking, aspect-preserving fitting and placement.
  It does not redraw, mirror, stretch or repair clinical relationships.
- Three assemblies retained: initial; two asset replacements with unchanged layout;
  then a separate mask/grounding revision without additional image calls.
- Total layered native calls: eleven, versus one for the baseline. Monetary cost
  was not exposed by the native lane; no dollar-cost estimate is claimed.

## Technical Findings

The observed native outputs were opaque RGB PNGs, not alpha cutouts. The generator's
initial note incorrectly called them JPEG; file inspection established PNG. A
magenta background enabled software masking, but near-key colors, trapped regions,
shadows and white sticker outlines required attention. Genuine transparency remains
unverified for this Antigravity lane.

Separate replacement worked: hashes confirm six untouched source assets and the
background remained identical when two groups were replaced. This is a real repair
control benefit. It does not prove either replaced group's clinical association.

Coordinate placement alone did not create a coherent room. Independently generated
support patches, differing scale/perspective, sticker outlines and floating objects
survived exact placement. Bottom alignment and reserved-hue masking improved the
assembly without paid generation, but did not solve all integration defects.

## Validation And Limits

Seven local compositor tests passed: fit, alpha preservation, conservative keying,
missing-key rejection, bounds, deterministic output/original preservation/aspect
rejection, and optional reserved-hue masking. These tests do not score image quality.
Programmatic checks verify eight unchanged VISUALs, matching final/baseline canvas
dimensions, the two intended asset replacements and unchanged background hash.

This is one adaptive pilot with unequal rendering effort, manually planned layout,
no shared background reference during asset generation, and a deliberately imperfect
lesson. It cannot establish first-pass reliability, equal-cost superiority, or
publication readiness. Complete-image independent audit results follow separately.

## Independent Results

Both reviewers inspected actual full-size pixels, all eight frozen anchors, and the
full saved source passages. Neither read the other's report or asked AG to grade.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Whole-scene baseline | 79 | 75 | 75, REPAIR |
| Initial layered assembly | 75 | 79 | 75, REPAIR |
| Two asset replacements plus mask/layout revision | 89 | 96 | 89, REPAIR |

Both found eight identifiable anchors in the final versus seven in the baseline.
Auditor A corrected an earlier inappropriate four-point penalty for a sanctioned
treatment metaphor; its baseline re-audit rose from 75 to 79 without a pixel change.
That correction is not a rendering improvement. Original reports remain preserved.

Final disagreement concerns whether the causes-category cue is sufficiently strong
and whether detached support surfaces are coherent room objects. Both retain the
ambiguous comparison association. We do not select the favorable 96 or average
away disagreement: the lower 89 governs. The rubric has fixed arithmetic, but its
semantic judgments demonstrably retain inter-rater variability.

The frozen lesson also retains source qualification and citation-entailment issues.
Even the favorable pixel-only PASS does not approve the clinical lesson for use.

## Decision

Worth continuing as a targeted repair/hybrid route, not adopting as the default
eight-cutout production renderer. Separate assets improve replaceability and
recovered a previously missing relationship. The first assembly did not outperform
the baseline conservatively, and useful improvement required extra generation and
adaptive composition work. Precision coordinates guarantee placement, not coherent
perspective, contact geometry, meaningful associations, or source accuracy.

Before production integration, test a source-approved lesson with a shared camera
and background reference, explicit floor/wall contact locations, consistent edge
treatment, and validated alpha/masks. Compare first outputs and bounded local repairs
on fresh topics, retaining failures and measuring native calls and human adjustments.
Do not introduce a final unconstrained AI repaint without re-auditing every anchor:
it could undo the very positional guarantees that motivated compositing.
