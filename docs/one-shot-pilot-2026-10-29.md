## Independent Findings

A non-author AI reviewer inspected the actual failed exports and outgoing/final
requests. This is independent AI review, not human medical expert review.
The reviewer also checked the six final outcomes and cost arithmetic against
saved artifacts and ledgers, finding no material factual error in this report.

- The writer was told VISUAL maximum 24 words, diagnostics say 30, and actual
  fatal validation is greater than 34. The failing 36-47-word fields exceed all
  three, so their rejection is not a false positive. Aligning messages alone
  will not solve generator nonadherence.
- The opening writer was explicitly told 20-35 words; the 66-word baseline
  opening violated that instruction as well as the downstream 60-word ceiling.
  Early story acceptance did not enforce the later opening boundary. The
  planner's separate 60-100-word scene_logic instruction is not a looser final
  opening allowance.
- The critic's strict tool schema did not prevent cross-field quote errors.
  Diverticulitis label complaints were incorrectly routed as clinical ANCHOR
  findings. Abnormal uterine bleeding also had a substantive object-count versus
  teaching-text inconsistency; correcting quote formatting alone would not clear
  that lesson. Preserve the block rather than silently rerouting or accepting it.
- One saved upstream scene suggestion specified a precise clock state despite
  the writer's precision-clock prohibition. This proves conflicting guidance,
  not that the conflict caused the length failures.

## Decision

Do not promote the candidate or claim improved one-shot reliability. This trial
isolates first-attempt failure; it does not evaluate the normal repair-enabled
production path, and three pairs cannot establish general reliability.

The next scoped development target is one consistent generation/review contract:
align emitted fields, actual limits and diagnostics; prevent planner suggestions
from contradicting active scene rules; distinguish clinical defects from visual
association defects while retaining exact attributed evidence. Replay the saved
failures offline before commissioning any further paid cohort. Do not loosen the
image rubric, clip medical text or spend the unused balance chasing these topics.
