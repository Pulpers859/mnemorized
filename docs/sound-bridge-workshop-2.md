## Unchanged Selection Standard

Whole terms/headwords only; one or two familiar drawable principal objects; no required labels, logos, proper-name faces or invisible actions. Do not hide missing stressed syllables, consonant edits or major vowel changes. Exact sound alone is insufficient when the object is likely to be named differently. Homophones, shared-word/polysemous cues and constructed rebuses remain separate types. Generic headwords do not recover missing medical modifiers.

These are AI editorial judgments, not validated psychometric thresholds or human clinical signoff. Proposals were generated independently of proprietary mnemonic catalogs; novelty is not guaranteed. All candidate render/recall evidence lists remain empty. Before production admission, independently source the exact lesson concept, test label-free naming and full-term/distractor recovery, then evaluate first images and delayed recall separately. No one of those checks substitutes for another.
