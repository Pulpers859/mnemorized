## Meaning And Evidence Boundaries

This is a candidate vocabulary bank, not a Forge lesson, clinical narration,
image-generation prompt or treatment guide. The boy/judge cues the law's name;
it does not replace learning its conditions and relationship. An empty oxygen
tank cues low oxygen, not a measured blood value or a diagnosis of its cause.
Clamps, balloons, bolts and taps are metaphorical props, not literal pathology
or instructions. No clinical mechanism or anatomical representation is declared
validated by source term presence. Detailed teaching still requires Forge source
retrieval and the Medical Quality Gate.
