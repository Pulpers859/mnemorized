## Reuse and Limits

The [broadened standard](chapter-1-broadened-cue-audit.md) and
[user calibration](chapter-2-cue-audit.md#subsequent-user-calibration) admit
familiar categories, actions, objects, metaphors and partial learned sound/name
bridges. Unique image-to-diagnosis identification in isolation is not required.
Earlier available chapter banks, the catalog and both sound-bridge workshops
were searched for reuse. The concurrent Chapter11 bank is outside this worker's
ownership and is not treated as an audited dependency.

Seven existing cues/primitives are explicitly reused. Cardiogenic shock is a
new composition of the catalog pump primitive, not a newly invented primitive.
The hidden curtain deliberately overlaps latent phase; context must supply
cryptic shock, and this does not assert clinical equivalence. Low-volume,
obstruction and heart-pump tokens similarly stay at family/name scope, not
pathophysiology. Vise/tube and refill tube are vocabulary sketches, not vascular
mechanism or examination diagrams. Passive leg raising names an action without
teaching how, when or why to perform it.

Milk supplies only lact-, crystals only crystall-, and a ringing bell only
Ringer. None must encode every syllable. Fresh separated egg white supplies a
learned albumen/albumin association without asserting chemistry or an infusion's
source. It must stay visibly distinct from the user's rotten-eggs/stink-lines
sulfur/sulfon primitive, which is not reassigned. No sulfur-family target is
selected here. The known blue-color collision between methylene blue and Prussian
blue remains contextual, not a reason to demand standalone diagnosis from color;
no blue drug target is selected here. Orange baking-soda packaging follows the
user-calibrated Chapter2 decision, not the narrower legacy catalog objection.
Lusitropy stays held rather than acquiring a forced multi-syllable rebus.

No proprietary composition, signature character system or copied mnemonic design
is proposed. Candidate usefulness is heuristic; source occurrence and reuse
