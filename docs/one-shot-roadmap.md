## Executable Envelope

Evidence-enabled normal success path: clinical extraction, independent contract
review, story writing, independent source/visual preflight. The medical quality
gate is a separate retrieval/check endpoint, not an Anthropic generation call.
Normal production has repairs and retries; the experimental profile must not.
Use6144rather than silently reducing the existing story output ceiling to6000.
At pinned Sonnet4.6 standard/global rates ($3/$15 per million input/output tokens),
24reservations of30000input+6144output are$4.37184. This is an estimated envelope,
NOT a proven dollar bound, and the$6task-wide budget is not increased.

Official token counting is described as an estimate. A margin and post-response
usage check cannot guarantee the triggering request stays below a dollar cap.
Dry-run enforcement is useful but must not be mislabeled live financial safety.
Until a defensible billable-input bound or a verified non-overshooting provider
cap exists, live execution stays blocked. Do not ask to refill or spend anyway.

## Freeze And Measurement

Use the existing first_pass_evaluation ledger, two arm ledgers linked by one
pairing manifest (one ledger rejects duplicate normalized topic prompts). Freeze
both before either arm generates. Same topic list/source policy/rubric/models;
predeclare randomized order and no pre-render repairs. Capture profile/requests
in real Forge exports. No synthetic medical scripts, recycled topics or prompt
rebuilding after export. Use visual_qa_pack.py without replacing exact prompt2.

Both independent auditors must score>=96/PASS with all eight units and source
clearance. Use the same frozen identity/relationship witnesses, normal1024px view
and native inspection. Keep old scores unchanged and report disagreements, not
an average. Three paired topics are a feasibility result, not proof of general
one-shot reliability. Do not rerun failures or spend leftover budget on repairs.

All local experiments/evidence: Troubleshooting Prompts/roadmap_2026-10-28/.
No image/recall gains may be claimed from software tests or this roadmap.
