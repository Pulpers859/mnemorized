## Reuse and Limits

Method follows the [broadened review](chapter-1-broadened-cue-audit.md) and
[subsequent user calibration](chapter-2-cue-audit.md#subsequent-user-calibration).
Familiar categories, actions, relationships and partial learned name associations
count; unique isolated image-to-diagnosis identification is not required.
Neutrons uses neutral as a learned fragment, not a particle explanation. Blue is
deliberately only a partial bridge for Prussian blue. Main flagged its shared
blue primitive with Chapter8 methylene blue: color alone cannot distinguish the
two. Keep lesson context; if both occur in one lesson, an additional distinguishing
cue is needed before production use. This is not a reason to reject the learned
family token here. Gray uses the ordinary color
word without claiming that grayness conveys a measurement. Becquerel remains
held without assembling strained syllables.

Available earlier chapter banks, the canonical catalog and both sound-bridge
workshops were checked for reusable terms/primitives. Shield, inside/outside and
low-quantity primitives already exist. New compositions are distinguished from
whole-cue reuses. Chapter6's user-supplied rotten-eggs association is deliberately
shared across sulfur/sulfon-related names: sulfadiazine is not assigned a novel
drug-specific picture. The eggs do not assert odor, chemistry, drug equivalence,
allergy relationships or treatment selection. The prior iodine workshop's held/
rejected eye-and-dine rebus was not revived to pad coverage.

Contamination markers are artificial markers, not visible radiation. The paired
inside/outside cues must not be conflated with irradiation alone. Fallout specks
are a word/action cue, not environmental physics. Device drawings are category
tokens, not operating instructions or detection specifications. The shield says
neither which material to use nor how much protection it gives. Peeling must stay
distinct from Chapter7's glove-shaped outer-cover removal. Pancytopenia and
dicentrics need learned token recognition; no counts, assays or dose estimates
are encoded. No clinical recommendations, doses, clinical mechanism claims,
proprietary scenes or signature character systems are proposed.
