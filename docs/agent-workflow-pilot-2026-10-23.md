# Lower-Usage Agent Workflow Pilot

## Verdict

**The lower-usage workflow did not achieve equivalent cue-authoring quality in this pilot. Source-head extraction was much closer.** Use smaller agents provisionally for bounded extraction/search assistance and structured bookkeeping, with deterministic checks. Keep cue selection, ambiguous holds, provenance adjudication and final admission under stronger review. Do not adopt cheaper end-to-end chapter authorship on this evidence.

This is a tiny lexical/editorial experiment, not a clinical, image, pronunciation, learner-recall or statistical-equivalence study. No new cue was admitted to the production catalog.

The70-unit source-head reference was fixed before outputs and hidden from authors.

- Lower-usage author: `gpt-6-luna`, medium reasoning.
- Stronger baseline author: `gpt-6.1-sol`, high reasoning.

- One initial attempt and at most one repair per arm. First artifacts/scores preserved. No further tuning after the planned repair.

Model names/efforts are the locally available agent settings, not published pricing claims. Both initial outputs passed schema, target and selected-reference checks.

## Repairs And Caveats

The lean repair addressed all17 original findings but introduced two residual defects: a low wall/sheet claimed to encode a basement without specifying a visible basement setting, and a Catamenial history note citing a selected cat cue as the older held cat/coal attempt. Active component refs were valid. The basement finding is an editorial scope/depiction judgment, not measured recognition failure; missing modifiers or incomplete syllables are not automatic rejections. No second repair or silent rescoping was allowed.

Both authors extracted the generic Miscellaneous organizer. The baseline also extracted passed, judged procedural scaffolding. Its primary first-pass precision therefore narrowly missed the predeclared98% threshold; admitting that literal action would give90/91 (98.90%). Both the primary result and this granularity sensitivity remain recorded. No first-pass criterion was relaxed to make the baseline perfect.

The lean raw repair was an explicit delta overlay rather than a complete replacement JSON. Main mechanically materialized its declared17 entry replacements and two extraction changes against immutable first output. No neural/content repair was added. Patch-versus-full repair format had been under-specified, so conversion burden is disclosed rather than assigned wholly to model quality. Five self-reported unmatched refs were compound-string parser limitations; semicolon-aware checks found all active refs present/nonheld.

Approximate wordplay, specialized-object recognition (notably aspergillum), hold judgment and anatomical-model familiarity remain uncertain. A single same-family AI judge may have style/model bias despite anonymous labels; concrete source/prior findings were corroborated, but no human assessment or recall experiment occurred.

## Efficiency

First output files appeared about55 and255 seconds after a shared dispatch checkpoint for lower/stronger authors respectively. These are artifact-readiness proxies: checkpoint followed launch calls, file creation is not verified final inference completion, runs overlapped, and review/setup time is excluded. Author notes report two versus seven prior searches. The lean draft was smaller (17,993 versus23,487 bytes).

**Exact per-agent tokens, quota weights and billed cost were not observable. No dollar or usage-savings percentage is claimed.** Codex account usage was consumed; there were no Anthropic/provider API calls or image renders. The separately authorized$6 Anthropic benchmark budget was untouched.

The lean arm needed17 semantic/provenance rechecks, versus zero baseline cue-entry rechecks; both needed extraction cleanup. Shared preparation and stronger review are real costs. The experimental initial audit was itself too verbose (about100KB JSON), then the delta report was bounded to exceptions. This pilot does not establish net savings for the full workflow.

## What This Supports

Use lower-usage agents on already-bounded source excerpts to extract heads and retrieve candidate prior records. Use scripts for exact-reference validation, deduplication, schema, counts and explicit patch application. Give a stronger curator the compact source/term/cue evidence and exception list for all high-judgment decisions. Report exceptions rather than reproducing the whole corpus in audit logs.

Do not infer that a smaller model independently discovers PDF chapter boundaries, handles arbitrary typography/OCR, makes final cue decisions or reduces total spend at equivalent quality. Source cropping/native extraction and the prior corpus were prepared once for both arms. Broader chapter/workflow reliability remains untested. Another cohort is not automatically authorized by this result.
