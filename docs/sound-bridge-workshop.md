# Sound-Bridge Workshop: October 19, 2026

## Honest Result

We workshopped **104 attempts covering 103 distinct medical terms/headwords**. Three AI proposal lanes plus main curation produced 32 shortlisted attempts (including a final eight-headword homonym batch); an independent AI reviewer retained **11 untested candidates**, held 16, and rejected five of those shortlisted attempts. The remaining attempts were rejected by their proposal lanes. Across unique terms the final result is 11 retained candidates, 16 holds, and 76 rejects. Duct was proposed twice; it is not two new words.

**This did not achieve 50 defensible additions.** The 103-term workshop is a research/rejection bank, not 103 usable cues. Eleven new entries are in the offline cue catalog, now 72 entries total. Only two are constructed rebuses (organ and rabies); two are whole-word homophones (palate and wheal), and seven are shared-word homonyms (palm, corn, globe, crown, bridge, nail, cone). No medication bridge survived this review for catalog admission. This bounded result is not a claim that better medication bridges or 50 good terms are impossible.

All are untested. No Anthropic calls, image renders, learner tests or new clinical lesson content were generated. Nothing is automatically injected into Forge. The separately authorized $6 Anthropic benchmark budget is untouched.

Admission decisions are editorial heuristics, not validated psychometric thresholds. The four pre-existing sound entries were not re-adjudicated in this workshop; their earlier candidate status does not establish that they satisfy this review's stricter selection judgments.

## Method And Resources

The three proposal lanes covered anatomy/procedures/devices, signs/diagnoses/labs, and medication names/classes. They saved first batches before extending them. A final bounded eight-item batch tested shared-word headwords; four were retained and four held. It did not change any earlier decision or produce more constructed rebuses. Reviewers were AI agents, not credentialed human specialists. Main integrated the stricter independent decisions without negotiating a larger yield. Raw artifacts and the independent review are saved under `Troubleshooting Prompts/sound_bridge_workshop_2026-10-19/`; that ignored packet is not required to use the tracked workshop bank.

The [ABEM EM Model](https://www.abem.org/resources/em-model/) and local `tools/tintinalli_manifest.json` informed broad curriculum relevance, not measured cross-session frequency. The [ABEM model PDF](https://www.abem.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/2022-EM-Model-for-Website.pdf) includes globe terminology in its ophthalmologic trauma section. The manifest was read for chapter topics/tags only; no claim of PDF ingestion or clinical source validation is made.

Selected NCI target forms were checked: [organ](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/organ) OR-gun, [palate](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/palate) PAL-et, [rabies](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/rabies) RAY-beez; also held [trachea](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/trachea), [iris](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/iris), [colon](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/colon), and rejected [ileum](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/ileum). The ileum check contradicted an initial EYE-based draft; it was corrected, not used as evidence of a good bridge. Individual NCI pages attempted for muscle, tic, wheal and ilium were unavailable, not treated as verified. Coma was checked but its proposal was not promoted.

Public Collins entries for [wheal](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/wheal), [palm](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/palm), [corn](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/corn), and [globe](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/globe) were checked for spoken forms/headwords; palm and corn list both relevant senses. Collins' globe entry supports the object/headword, not anatomical meaning. The proposed word-image pairings remain our untested design judgments; dictionaries do not endorse them. For the final retained headwords, Collins also lists the relevant shared senses for [crown](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/crown), [bridge](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/bridge), [nail](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/nail), and [cone](https://www.collinsdictionary.com/us/dictionary/english/cone). Root/socket/arch/rod pages were opened but their proposals remain held; no source check changes that decision. The whole bank was not dictionary-audited. No proprietary mnemonic catalog, scene or signature character system was imported; independent proposals are not guaranteed novel.

The JSON attempts preserve proposal-stage wording, including statements that a term had not yet been dictionary-checked. Later main-agent checks listed above supersede that historical metadata, not the untested status. The anatomy draft's phrase "User reports verified NCI" was a mistaken attribution: the check was performed by the main AI agent, not Patrick. Raw artifacts are preserved rather than silently rewritten to conceal that error.

## What This Suggests

Pure sound play is a selective tool, not a sensible mandatory encoding for every medical term. Long medication names were especially vulnerable to missing syllables, altered stress, obscure props or labels. Use a source-grounded direct visual association when no clean sound bridge exists. Do not turn a plausible phonetic fragment into a claim of full drug-name recognition.

Before any later admission or production use: freeze the subset and target pronunciation; test unprompted object naming, whole-term recovery, wrong-domain responses and plausible near-name distractors; then independently inspect first renders and measure delayed recall separately. Neither a coached explanation nor a repaired image is first-pass evidence.
